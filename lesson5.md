AI Avatar
„bar bezahlen“ means to pay in cash.

bar = in cash
bezahlen = to pay
„hätte“
The base verb is haben = to have. Hätte is a polite form meaning would have and is used in „Ich hätte gern …“ = I would like …

German	English
ich hätte	I would have
du hättest	you would have
er/sie/es hätte	he/she/it would have
wir hätten	we would have
ihr hättet	you would have
Sie/sie hätten	you/they would have
For ordering, say:

Ich hätte gern eine Tageskarte.
= I would like a day ticket.

The seller asked: Möchten Sie bar bezahlen?
= Would you like to pay in cash?
